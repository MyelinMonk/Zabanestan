Classes, study groups and everyday questions happen on our Discord server. Say hello in the server and the team can help you there.

What to include

A few details help us answer sooner.

Volunteering
Which language you speak or want to learn, and how you’d like to help. Get involved lists where help counts most.
Corrections
The page and what needs changing, with a source if you have one. Changes to the roster are made by the core team.
Suggesting a resource
A full citation: author, title, year and publisher.
Takedown requests
The material and where it appears. We review the request, take the material down and reply within 7 days.
The reader
What you were doing when something went wrong, and which device and browser you use. See also the reader guide.
