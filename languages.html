Resource pages for each language are currently undergoing maintenance and will be available in the near future. Endangerment levels follow UNESCO’s scale and draw on the UNESCO atlases, Glottolog and Encyclopaedia Iranica. Codes are ISO 639-3. Last reviewed {{ site.roster_reviewed }}.

Other languages we study

Members also study languages of the wider Persianate world that don’t meet our endangerment criteria: Tajik, Tehrani Persian, Azerbaijani, Qashqai, Shirazi and Elamite. Members can study them with us, but they aren’t a priority for our public materials.
