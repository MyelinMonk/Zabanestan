What we post, and what we don’t

We publish only:

  • our own work, under CC BY-SA 4.0
  • works in the public domain
  • openly licensed works, on their own terms
  • material we have written permission to share

We don’t post scans or files of copyrighted books, and we don’t link to sites that host them without permission. Our own materials take facts from published sources and explain them in our own words, citing every source.

To ask us to remove your own material, email {{ site.email }}. We review the request, take the material down and reply within 7 days.
