Get involved

Zabanestan runs on volunteers. These are the jobs where help counts most right now.

Speak one of our languages
Record words, stories or conversations with us. You decide how you’re credited, who can hear the recording and what it’s used for. Fill in our consent form to get started, or read how recording works.
Lead a language
We’re looking for a lead for each language on our roster, ideally a native or heritage speaker, to set priorities and check what we publish.
Teach or study
We teach classes in the voice channels of our Discord server. Join a class or study group, or teach one if you know a language well.
Check rights
Help confirm that everything we publish is ours to share, and replace what isn’t with citations and legitimate links.
Look after the archive
Keep our metadata and backups in order, and prepare collections for deposit in a language archive.
Email us

Write to {{ site.email }} and tell us which language you speak or want to learn, and how you’d like to help.

Recording with us

Before we record, we explain what will be recorded, where it will be stored and published, and who can access it. You give consent in writing, or aloud at the start of the recording, and you can skip any prompt or stop at any time.

Credit
Your full name, a name you choose, or no name at all.
Access
Open to anyone, restricted to researchers or community members who ask us, or kept private for preservation only.
Uses
You pick the uses you agree to: lessons, dictionaries, an archive deposit, Wikimedia projects or speech technology.
Changing your mind
You can change your choices or withdraw at any time. We take the recording down from our sites and ask any archive holding it to restrict access.

If speaking your language publicly could put you at risk, anonymity is the default. We show no faces, names or places unless you choose otherwise, we strip location data from files, and we never attach political commentary to anything you contribute.
