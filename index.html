We document, preserve and teach endangered Iranic languages

Zabanestan is a volunteer community focused on {{ language_count }} languages, from Zazaki in eastern Anatolia to Wakhi in the Pamirs. We work with speakers, and everything we make is free to use and share.

Get involved See the {{ language_count }} languages

What we make

Every finished piece is published somewhere built to last.

Learning materials
Lessons, vocabulary decks, learning software and bilingual readers, written by our team and openly licensed.
Reference resources
Bibliographies, word lists and dictionaries built from several sources and checked with speakers.
Documentation
Recordings of speakers with transcriptions and translations, made with consent and deposited in a recognized archive.
Translation projects
Classical and modern texts brought into English.
Community
Classes, study groups and social media, where members learn, practice and discuss together.

How we work

Speakers first

Native speakers and heritage communities shape decisions about their own languages, and they’re credited for their work.

Consent before recording

No one is recorded, quoted or credited without informed consent. Speakers choose how they’re named and who can hear them.

Open by default

What we create is free to use and share under CC BY-SA 4.0, and we don’t post other people’s copyrighted work without permission.

Built to last

Finished work goes to archives and open platforms that will outlast any one of us and any single platform.
